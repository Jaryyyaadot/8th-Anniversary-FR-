Chapter one · Middle school, 2018

The first spark

Ja-O felt it the very first moment she saw him — something close to love at first sight. But she kept it locked away, playing it cool, like it was nothing at all.

Mon, back then, was a little lost in himself — quiet, nonchalant, like nothing could quite reach him. Not even a girl who had already decided he was worth noticing.

Chapter two · Somewhere along the way

Strawberry lollipops

Then one day, it was always strawberry. He'd noticed, quietly, that it was her favorite — and never said a word about why. Ja-O felt seen for the first time.
